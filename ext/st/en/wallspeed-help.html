Wallspeed

Wallspeed is Speedtab’s wallpaper and appearance space. Its galleries can be updated without changing your local workspace.

Wallpaper

Choose an image and select As Wallpaper. Speedtab downloads it, stores it locally as a background asset, and applies it to your workspace.

Colors

Color and gradient presets are applied directly as your workspace background.

Reset

Reset restores the default Speedtab wallpaper and dark appearance.

Picture-in-Picture

Open Wallspeed in Picture-in-Picture to keep browsing wallpapers in a separate window.
