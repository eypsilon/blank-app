Wallspeed

Speedtab’s live appearance space. Browse, preview, and apply styles directly—all galleries stay updated remotely without modifying your local workspace setup.

Wallpaper

Select As Wallpaper on any item. Speedtab fetches the high-res file, caches it locally, and updates your active workspace background.

* Saved wallpapers are stored locally and remain accessible anytime via the Assets manager.

Colors

Instant solid color and gradient presets applied directly to your canvas without heavy asset loading.

Picture-in-Picture

Pop Wallspeed out into a floating window to test and preview wallpapers side-by-side with your active tabs.

Reset

Instantly restores Speedtab’s original dark theme and default wallpaper state.
